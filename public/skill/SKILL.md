# Shanghai architectural cutaway

Create an informative architectural reading image that lets a viewer understand a Shanghai landmark's exterior identity, internal space, structural logic, and urban setting at once.

## Before generating

1. Verify the building's official name, location, architectural period, distinguishing exterior form, and publicly documented program. Prefer official operator, museum, municipal, or preservation sources.
2. Decide what one view must explain. Select a three-quarter axonometric section, a longitudinal section, or a transverse section based on the clearest spatial story. Do not automatically cut through the geometric center.
3. List only the internal facts that public sources reasonably support. If full construction drawings are unavailable, use a plausible conceptual spatial reading and label the work accordingly.

## Image contract

- Preserve a recognizable exterior silhouette, material palette, and immediate city context.
- Remove one façade or wedge and selectively lift roof pieces. Keep the cut line legible with a restrained warm cut-face color.
- Reveal structural system, circulation, principal rooms, and one city relationship. A dense building does not require every floor to be illustrated.
- Use warm ivory paper, precise ink linework, restrained watercolor, portrait 3:4 composition, and original artwork. Do not request an imitation of a living artist or existing drawing.
- Include the building title plus six to eight concise, correctly spelled Chinese callouts. Ensure every label points to a visible feature.
- Add no brand marks, watermark, close-up people, invented historical styles, or photorealistic rendering.

## Prompt workflow

Read [the prompt template](references/prompt-template.md), replace every bracketed field, then generate one image per building. Inspect the result for: title accuracy, legible callouts, silhouette recognition, a meaningful cut axis, and contradictions between labels and visible spaces.

## Honesty boundary

Describe the output as an **architectural reading illustration** or **conceptual cutaway**. It is not a measured survey, construction drawing, heritage assessment, fire plan, or authoritative record of inaccessible interiors.
